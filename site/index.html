The Elderwold

How to play

Move with A/D or ←/→. Jump with Space/W/↑. J strikes, K heavy attacks, L dodges, U throws stones. Tap I to spend 25 Rage on a directional bear skill; hold I for a full 90-second transformation. E interacts with dens. Find the Den, light the Deep Stone, and reach Old Thorngut.

Enemies tell you what comes next. Dodge the lunge, then punish the recovery. The man is fragile; the bear's wounds are paid when the shape breaks. Trees felled by the bear remain as bridges.
