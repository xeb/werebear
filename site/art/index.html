Visual DNA: The Man & The Beast

In accordance with GAME.md lore and the user's direction, the human protagonist shares the identical visual lineage with the Primalist Titan:

  • Bone Totem Chest Rigging: Carved prehistoric tusks & runes bound by weathered leather straps across the torso.
  • Talisman Belt & Pouches: Runic bone plaques hanging from leather thongs over a ragged fur loincloth.
  • Glowing Amber Eyes: The spiritual fire of the shape-strong burns in both human and beast forms.
  • Ash & Charcoal Tones: Human hair and werebear coat share matching dark charcoal with weathered highlights.

Engine Integration Pipeline

Next Steps for Illustrated Atlas Integration

Per GAME.md §12.2 (Illustrated sprite pipeline from readysetsanta), these 3D character renders will be cut out to transparent PNGs, packed onto power-of-two sprite atlases with foot baseline registration, and wired into the game's 60 Hz deterministic simulation loop:

1. Transparent Alpha Cutout

Use background extraction / connected-components to produce transparent WebP/PNG sprites aligned to a shared foot baseline.

2. Atlas Packing (4×3 Grid)

Pack the 38 human poses and 26 bear poses onto compact sprite sheets with hand-calibrated hitboxes and weapon sockets.

3. Procedural Motion & Juice

Combine key poses with procedural lean, squash/stretch, hit-stop, and camera zoom-out as specified in §12.2 and §7.5.
